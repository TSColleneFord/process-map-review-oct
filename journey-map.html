Client Journey Map

How a SNAP recipient moves from being found eligible, or being identified by a provider, to starting and finishing SNAP E&T services. The participant is at the center: their steps run along the top lane with a heavier border, and every other role is shown only as far as it shapes what the participant experiences. This reflects state staff descriptions from the October 6 working session. Participant experiences and provider steps have not yet been validated directly with participants or providers.

Scroll or drag sideways to follow the journey. The lettered stages are columns that run down through every role, and their headers stay pinned at the top as you read down. Participant steps are numbered in order; the three doors are all step 1, because a person uses one of them, and each door’s route keeps its own color until it joins the main path. Icons on a step show what we heard there; select any step or connector for the full detail. To give feedback, select a step or connector and choose Comment; a short form opens with that spot already filled in.
