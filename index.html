SNAP E&T Process Maps

Final October 2026 Prepared by Third Sector Capital Partners

Two maps of how SNAP Employment and Training works in Louisiana today: how a participant moves through the program, and how providers are paid for the services they deliver. Both were built from interviews and working sessions with LDH, Louisiana Works, and provider staff, then revised with staff feedback.
